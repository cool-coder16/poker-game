How scoring works
  • Singleplayer runs. A run starts with 1,000 chips, has no rebuys, and ends the moment anyone at the table goes bankrupt. Blinds start at 10/20 and rise every 10 hands. Your run score is the chips you have left (0 if you went bankrupt).
  • Weekly season. Every Monday (UTC) the season resets. Your season score is your best run that week.
  • Qualifying. Play at least 30 hands in the week to get a rank. Players under 30 hands are listed below the line.
  • bb/100. Big blinds won per 100 hands. It is the standard poker skill number.
  • Rating points. When a week ends, your final place turns into points that add up forever: 1st = 100, 2nd = 70, 3rd = 50, 4th-10th = 30, everyone else who qualified = 10.
  • Arena. Finish in the top 3 of an Arena (see the Arena page) to earn 3 / 2 / 1 rating points. You need at least 2 rounds played, and the Arena needs at least 3 such players. They are counted the next time you open the site after the Arena ends.
  • Tournaments. Finish 1st / 2nd / 3rd in a Sit & Go or scheduled tournament to earn 5 / 3 / 1 rating points (a 3-player one pays 3 / 1 / 0). At least 3 human players must have started; bots never earn points.
  • High scores. Your single best run ever, whatever the week. Practice mode never counts.
  • Fair play. Chips are play money only; there are no prizes that cost or pay real money. Games run in your browser for now, so the board works on the honor system until multiplayer is server-run.
