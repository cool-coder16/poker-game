    How the Arena works
    • Every half hour (at :00 and :30, UTC) a new 30-minute Arena starts. Join any time while it's running.
    • Rounds. You sit at a table with other players. Everyone starts each round with the same chips and plays 10 hands, with the blinds rising every 3 hands.
    • Points. Your finishing place in the round pays 2 points for every player you beat, plus 2 bonus points for winning. In a 6-player round that's 12, 8, 6, 4, 2, 0.
    • No elimination. Bust and you simply wait for the next round. Your Arena score is the total of your round points across every table.
    • Rating. When an Arena ends, the top 3 earn 3 / 2 / 1 rating points (you need at least 2 rounds played, and the Arena needs at least 3 such players). They are added to your rating the next time you open the site, and appear on your Account page.
    • Tables. "Join the Arena" seats you at the fullest table with room, or starts a new one. If you join mid-round you play from the next round.
    • Fair play. Chips are play money, with no prizes that cost or pay real money. A table's dealer reports its scores from their browser, so the standings run on the honor system for now.
