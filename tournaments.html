        How tournaments work
        • Freezeout. Everyone starts with 1,000 chips. Blinds start at 10/20 and rise every 5 hands. There are no rebuys: when you run out of chips you're out, and the last player standing wins.
        • Rating points. Finishing 1st / 2nd / 3rd earns 5 / 3 / 1 rating points (a 3-player tournament pays 3 / 1 / 0). At least 3 human players must have started, and bots never earn points. Your points are added the next time you open the site after it finishes.
        • One table. These are single-table tournaments of up to 8 players. Real multi-table tournaments need a server to move players between tables, which is a later step.
        • The dealer. One player's browser deals for the table. If they leave or disconnect, another player takes over. The results run on the honor system for now.
